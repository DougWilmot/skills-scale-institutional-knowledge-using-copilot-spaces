# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The QA Lead defines the testing strategy and quality gates for the project. They ensure that software quality, testability, and acceptance criteria are treated as first-class outcomes across the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy aligned with project scope and risk profile
- Lead the creation of test plans, test cases, and automation frameworks
- Establish quality gates and acceptance testing criteria
- Manage QA resources and coordinate testing across project phases
- Report quality metrics, defects, and release readiness to the PM and PdM
- Collaborate with developers on testability during design and implementation
- Escalate quality risks and blockers that threaten delivery or customer outcomes

### Goals
- Improve delivery confidence and product quality
- Reduce defects escaping into release
- Align quality practices with business and technical risk

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy reviews and release readiness discussions
- QA status updates in standups and weekly project reviews
- Collaboration with developers on bug triage and validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to improve test coverage and validate implementation quality
- Partners with Product Managers to translate priorities and acceptance criteria into testable requirements
- Reports quality status and risk to Project Managers to support planning and release decisions
- Helps identify gaps between business expectations and operational quality

---

## Technical Lead / Technical Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Technical Architect provides technical direction, standards, and design guidance. They ensure the solution remains scalable, maintainable, and aligned with the project’s technical constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review designs and code for architectural compliance
- Identify and manage technical risks, trade-offs, and dependencies
- Establish engineering standards, coding best practices, and technical guardrails
- Mentor developers and guide technical decision-making
- Plan for scalability, performance, and maintainability
- Escalate technical blockers or design risks to the right stakeholders

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound and maintainable solutions
- Reduce avoidable complexity and rework
- Support sustainable engineering practices across the project

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical planning sessions
- Design discussions with developers and project leads
- Escalation updates when technical risks affect scope or timeline

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers on architecture, implementation trade-offs, and engineering practices
- Advises Project Managers on technical feasibility, dependencies, and risks
- Supports Product Managers in assessing the practical impact of roadmap or scope decisions
- Coordinates with QA Lead on testing strategies for complex or high-risk components

---

## Security Champion

### Role Summary
The Security Champion ensures security is built into the product and process. They own security practices, vulnerability review, and risk mitigation across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and threat models
- Review designs and implementation for security vulnerabilities
- Manage security scanning, compliance checks, and risk assessments
- Lead security incident response and post-incident reviews
- Establish security standards and promote secure-by-default practices
- Drive awareness of secure development and deployment practices
- Escalate security risks, dependencies, or compliance issues

### Goals
- Reduce security-related product and operational risk
- Embed security into design, engineering, and release workflows
- Improve response readiness for incidents or vulnerabilities

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews with engineering and release stakeholders
- Risk updates in team syncs and escalation paths
- Incident coordination with the PM and broader leadership when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers on secure coding standards and remediation guidance
- Works with QA Lead on security testing and validation checkpoints
- Advises Project Managers on security timelines, dependencies, and release risk
- Reports critical issues and mitigation status to Sponsors or executive stakeholders when needed

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst bridges strategy and delivery. They translate stakeholder needs into clear requirements, ensure business logic is understood, and help keep the project aligned to real customer and business value.

### Responsibilities
- Gather requirements through stakeholder interviews and analysis
- Document business needs, workflows, and process context
- Define clear, testable acceptance criteria
- Create and maintain traceability between business needs and implementation
- Validate that delivered work meets business intent
- Identify scope changes and their impact on outcomes or delivery
- Clarify ambiguity between business and technical teams

### Goals
- Improve requirement clarity and alignment
- Reduce misunderstanding between stakeholders and the delivery team
- Support better product decisions and outcome delivery

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder meetings
- Clarification sessions with Product Managers and Developers
- Review of acceptance criteria and validation outcomes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to define priorities, outcomes, and requirements
- Helps Developers understand user workflows and business intent
- Supports QA Lead in creating test scenarios and validating business outcomes
- Keeps stakeholders informed about trade-offs and scope impacts

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
The Sponsor or Executive Stakeholder provides high-level direction, strategic alignment, and decision-making authority for the project. They ensure that the work remains connected to business priorities and organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic objectives, success criteria, and expected business impact
- Provide budget, staffing, and resource support
- Remove organizational blockers and resolve cross-functional issues
- Make prioritization decisions on competing initiatives
- Approve major scope changes, milestones, or trade-offs
- Monitor executive-level status, risks, and project value realization

### Goals
- Ensure the project advances agreed business outcomes
- Maintain strategic alignment and accountability
- Support the team with the resources and authority needed to succeed

### Typical Communication
- Executive updates and milestone reviews
- Escalation discussions when risks or strategic decisions are required
- Sponsor alignment meetings tied to business outcomes and delivery health

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receives status and risk updates from Project Managers
- Supports Product Managers and Project Managers during escalations or major trade-offs
- Provides context and authority for stakeholder alignment and organizational decisions
- Helps unblock dependencies that are beyond the project team’s control
